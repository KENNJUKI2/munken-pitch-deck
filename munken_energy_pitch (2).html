Why It Matters · 4/8

Why Battery Storage Is the Game-Changer

Solar alone isn't enough. BESS makes it dependable.

Without BESS
  • Exposed at night and during outages
  • Intermittent power = unreliable operations
  • High diesel generator dependency
  • Limited commercial viability
With BESS
  • Dependable 24/7 power system
  • Peak-shaving for commercial customers
  • Grid stabilization for weak rural lines
  • Full energy independence
BESS turns solar from a daytime luxury into a round-the-clock utility.

Business Model & Revenue · 6/8

Customers pay for power. Munken owns the infrastructure and the risk.

How Customers Pay

Outright purchase

Full payment upfront; customer owns the system immediately.

Lease-to-own

Fixed monthly payments to Munken over 24–48 months.

Pay-as-you-go

Mobile-money micro-payments tied to power actually used.

How Munken Earns

Installation margin

Earned on every system sold, across all six segments.

Financing payments

Interest built into lease-to-own and PAYG contracts.

Service & monitoring fees

Recurring monthly fees for uptime and maintenance.

This seed round buys the first wave of hardware and installations — letting Munken front the cost of lease-to-own and pay-as-you-go plans for schools, clinics, agri-co-ops, hotels, SMEs and households.
